Politika privatnosti

Info ekran · Poslednja izmena: 26. oktobar 2026.

Srpski · English version ↓

Info ekran je lični informativni ekran. Prikazuje vremensku prognozu i kvalitet vazduha, događaje iz jednog Google kalendara i jelovnik vrtića, a uz njega radi Telegram bot koji od pozivnica pravi događaje u tom kalendaru. Nije komercijalna usluga i ne prikazuje reklame. Ovde je opisano koje podatke stranica i pozadinski servis obrađuju, zašto i kome se šalju.

Važno: događaji iz kalendara se na stranici prikazuju svakome ko je otvori, bez prijave. Zato se u taj kalendar ne upisuju osetljivi podaci.

1. Ko je odgovoran

Stranicu i servis vodi vlasnik projekta kao privatno lice. Pitanja, uvid, ispravke i zahtevi za brisanje: day.info.screen@gmail.com.

2. Koje podatke obrađujemo

Kad otvorite stranicu

Vaš browser kontaktira nekoliko servisa (spisak je u odeljku 5). Svaki od njih vidi vašu IP adresu i osnovne podatke o browseru i uređaju. Sama stranica ne postavlja kolačiće i ne čuva ništa u browseru; kolačiće može da postavi Google Analytics (odeljak 6).

Lokacija

Ako dozvolite pristup lokaciji, browser šalje koordinate direktno servisu Open-Meteo da bi prikazao prognozu za vaše mesto. Koordinate se ne šalju na naš servis i ne čuvaju se. Ako odbijete, prikazuje se prognoza za Beograd.

Događaji iz kalendara

Stranica prikazuje naslov, datum, vreme i lokaciju događaja iz kalendara naloga day.info.screen@gmail.com za današnji dan i naredna tri. Taj prikaz nije zaštićen lozinkom.

Telegram bot

Bot odgovara samo odobrenim korisnicima, koji se prepoznaju po Telegram ID-ju. Obrađuje tekst i slike pozivnica koje mu pošaljete, kao i ID korisnika i čata (radi provere pristupa). Poruke ostalih korisnika se ignorišu; jedino na komandu /start dobijaju svoj ID, da bi mogli da zatraže pristup.

Slike

Slike pozivnica koje pošaljete botu i slika jelovnika sa sajta vrtića šalju se servisu freeocr.ai radi prepoznavanja teksta. Jelovnik je javno objavljen na sajtu vrtića.

3. Google korisnički podaci

  • Kome i čemu se pristupa. Aplikacija koristi dozvolu https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events (pregled i izmena događaja) i to samo za jedan Google nalog, day.info.screen@gmail.com. Taj nalog pripada vlasniku projekta, koji je sam odobrio pristup. Aplikacija nije namenjena drugim korisnicima Google naloga i ne traži njihove podatke.
  • Šta čitamo. Događaje za današnji dan i naredna tri (naslov, vreme, lokacija), radi prikaza na stranici.
  • Šta upisujemo. Događaje koje potvrdite dugmetom „Da“ u Telegram botu (naslov, vreme, lokacija i opis iz pozivnice). Ništa se ne upisuje bez potvrde.
  • Kako ih koristimo. Samo za ove dve funkcije. Ne prodajemo ih, ne koristimo ih za reklame ni profilisanje, ne koristimo ih za obuku modela veštačke inteligencije i ne prenosimo ih drugim stranama, osim što se pročitani događaji prikazuju na stranici (vidi gore). Ne čitaju ih ljudi, osim vlasnika projekta.
  • Zaštita. Pristupni token čuva se kao tajna promenljiva okruženja kod hosting provajdera, ne u kodu ni u repozitorijumu. Komunikacija ide šifrovanom vezom (HTTPS).
  • Čuvanje. Servis nema bazu podataka. Pročitani događaji drže se u memoriji do 5 minuta radi keširanja.
  • Povlačenje pristupa. Pristup možete da opozovete u bilo kom trenutku na myaccount.google.com/permissions. Događaji koje je bot upisao ostaju u kalendaru dok ih ne obrišete.

Korišćenje i prenos informacija dobijenih od Google API-ja pridržava se pravila Google API Services User Data Policy, uključujući zahteve o ograničenoj upotrebi (Limited Use).

6. Kolačići

Stranica i pozadinski servis ne postavljaju sopstvene kolačiće. Google Analytics može da postavi kolačiće radi merenja poseta. Možete da ih blokirate u podešavanjima browsera ili dodatkom za blokiranje; stranica radi i bez njih.

7. Vaši izbori i prava

  • Možete da odbijete pristup lokaciji; prikazuje se Beograd.
  • Možete da blokirate Google Analytics i kolačiće.
  • Pristup Google nalogu opozivate na myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Poruke poslate botu možete da obrišete u svom Telegram čatu.
  • Uvid, ispravku ili brisanje podataka možete da zatražite na day.info.screen@gmail.com.

8. Deca

Servis ne prikuplja namerno podatke o deci. Jelovnik vrtića je dokument koji vrtić javno objavljuje na svom sajtu.

9. Izmene ove politike

Ako se način rada promeni, ova stranica se ažurira, a datum na vrhu pokazuje poslednju izmenu.

Privacy Policy

Info ekran (“Info screen”) · Last updated: 26 October 2026

↑ Srpski · English

Info ekran is a personal information screen. It shows the weather forecast and air quality, events from a single Google Calendar, and a kindergarten lunch menu. A Telegram bot turns invitations into events in that calendar. It is not a commercial service and shows no ads. This policy describes what data the page and its backend service process, why, and who receives it.

Please note: calendar events are displayed on the page to anyone who opens it, without signing in. For that reason no sensitive information should be put in that calendar.

1. Who is responsible

The page and the service are run by the project owner as a private individual. Questions, access, correction and deletion requests: day.info.screen@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

When you open the page

Your browser contacts several services (listed in section 5). Each of them sees your IP address and basic browser and device information. The page itself sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser; Google Analytics may set cookies (section 6).

Location

If you allow location access, your browser sends the coordinates directly to Open-Meteo to get the forecast for your place. The coordinates are not sent to our service and are not stored. If you decline, the forecast for Belgrade is shown.

Calendar events

The page shows the title, date, time and location of events in the calendar of day.info.screen@gmail.com for today and the next three days. This display is not password protected.

Telegram bot

The bot responds only to approved users, identified by Telegram ID. It processes the text and images of invitations you send it, and the user and chat IDs (to check access). Messages from other users are ignored; they only receive their own ID in reply to /start, so they can request access.

Images

Invitation images sent to the bot, and the menu image from the kindergarten website, are sent to freeocr.ai for text recognition. The menu is published publicly on the kindergarten’s website.

3. Google user data

  • What is accessed. The application uses the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events (view and edit events) for one Google account only, day.info.screen@gmail.com, which belongs to the project owner, who granted the access. The application is not intended for other Google account holders and does not request their data.
  • What we read. Events for today and the next three days (title, time, location), to display them on the page.
  • What we write. Events you confirm with the “Yes” button in the Telegram bot (title, time, location and description taken from the invitation). Nothing is written without confirmation.
  • How we use it. Only for these two features. We do not sell it, do not use it for advertising or profiling, do not use it to train AI models, and do not transfer it to others, except that the events read are shown on the page (see above). No humans read it, other than the project owner.
  • Protection. The access token is stored as a secret environment variable at the hosting provider, not in code or in the repository. Communication uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Retention. The service has no database. Events read are kept in memory for up to 5 minutes (caching).
  • Revoking access. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events the bot created remain in the calendar until you delete them.

The use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Cookies

The page and the backend service set no cookies of their own. Google Analytics may set cookies to measure visits. You can block them in your browser settings or with a blocking extension; the page works without them.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can decline location access; Belgrade is shown instead.
  • You can block Google Analytics and cookies.
  • You can revoke Google account access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • You can delete messages you sent to the bot in your own Telegram chat.
  • To request access, correction or deletion of data, write to day.info.screen@gmail.com.

8. Children

The service does not knowingly collect data about children. The kindergarten menu is a document the kindergarten publishes openly on its own website.

9. Changes to this policy

If the way the service works changes, this page is updated and the date at the top shows the latest change.
